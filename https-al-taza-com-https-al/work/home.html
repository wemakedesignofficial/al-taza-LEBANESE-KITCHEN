Frequently Asked Questions

1. What makes Al Taza different from other shawarma brands?

At Al Taza, we fuse authentic Lebanese culinary traditions with modern Indian tastes. We use fresh, Grade-A halal meat, homemade sauces, and never compromise on hygiene. Our signature dishes like the Taza Royal Shawarma are handcrafted with daily-prepped ingredients and original spice blends.

2. How do you ensure hygiene and food safety in your kitchens?

All our outlets follow strict SOPs including surface sanitization every 2 hours, daily health checks for staff, and food-safe PPE gear. We use food-safe disinfectants and operate open kitchens for complete transparency.

3. Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes! We offer a wide range of vegetarian and vegan-friendly dishes including falafel rolls, hummus platters, soya shawarmas, and high-protein bowls with lentils or chickpeas.

4. Can I order online or for takeaway?

Absolutely. You can place orders via our website, mobile app, or through delivery partners like Swiggy and Zomato. We also offer fast takeaway options — most ready within 6–8 minutes.

5. Do you deliver to my location?

Delivery is available across most areas surrounding our outlets. Use our “Find Nearest Outlet” feature or check through delivery apps for coverage in your area.
